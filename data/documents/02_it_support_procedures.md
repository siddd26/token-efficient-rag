# Northstar Retail Systems — IT Support Procedures

Document ID: NRS-IT-002
Owner: IT Service Management
Effective date: 1 January 2026

## 1. Contacting the Service Desk

The IT Service Desk is the single point of contact for all IT issues and requests. Employees can log a ticket through the HelpPoint portal or call the Service Desk on internal extension 4357. The Service Desk is staffed from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Outside these hours, an on-call engineer can be reached through the after-hours line, which may be used for Priority 1 incidents only.

## 2. Ticket Priorities and Targets

Every ticket is assigned a priority level, which determines its response and resolution targets:

- Priority 1 (Critical): a store's point-of-sale (POS) system is down, or a company-wide outage. Response within 15 minutes; resolution target of 4 hours.
- Priority 2 (High): a single user cannot work, or a business-critical function is degraded. Response within 1 hour; resolution target of 1 business day.
- Priority 3 (Normal): an issue with a workaround available. Response within 4 business hours; resolution target of 3 business days.
- Priority 4 (Request): standard service requests such as new accessories. Response within 2 business days; resolution target of 10 business days.

## 3. Password Resets and Account Lockouts

Employees can reset a forgotten password themselves through the self-service option in the HelpPoint portal, which requires multi-factor authentication (MFA). After 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts, the account is automatically locked for 30 minutes. An employee who is locked out and cannot use self-service must call the Service Desk on extension 4357, provide their employee ID, and have their identity verified by their line manager before the account is unlocked.

## 4. Hardware Requests

New hardware must be requested through HelpPoint and requires line manager approval. Standard laptops from the approved hardware list are delivered within 5 business days of approval. Non-standard hardware requires additional approval from IT Procurement and is delivered within 15 business days of approval.

## 5. Software Installation

Employees may only install software listed in the Approved Software Catalogue, which is available in HelpPoint. To request software that is not in the catalogue, the employee must submit a Software Request ticket in HelpPoint with a business justification and line manager approval. The request is then sent to Information Security for a security review, which can take up to 10 business days. The software is installed by IT only if the security review is passed.

## 6. Lost or Stolen Devices

If a company laptop, phone, or tablet is lost or stolen, the employee must report it to the Service Desk within 1 hour of discovering the loss. The employee must also report it to Information Security as a security incident. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. If the device was stolen, the employee should also file a police report and add the report number to the HelpPoint ticket.

## 7. POS Terminal Failure Procedure

If a POS terminal fails in a store, store staff should follow these steps in order:

1. Restart the terminal using the power button and wait 2 minutes.
2. Check that the network cable is firmly connected to the terminal and the wall port.
3. Move customers to a working backup terminal.
4. If the terminal still does not work, log a ticket with the Service Desk. Log it as Priority 1 if more than 2 terminals in the store are affected; otherwise log it as Priority 2.

## 8. Returning Equipment

Employees leaving Northstar must return all company equipment, including laptops, phones, badges, and chargers, to IT within 5 working days of their last working day.
