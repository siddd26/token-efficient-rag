# Northstar Retail Systems — Information Security Rules

Document ID: NRS-SEC-005
Owner: Information Security
Effective date: 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

These rules protect Northstar's systems, customer data, and employee data. They apply to all employees, contractors, and temporary staff who use Northstar systems or handle Northstar information.

## 2. Passwords and Multi-Factor Authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords must be changed every 180 days, and immediately if a compromise is suspected. Employees may not reuse any of their last 10 passwords. Passwords must never be shared, including with managers or IT staff.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for company email, the HelpPoint portal, the PeopleHub portal, and the NorthLink VPN.

## 3. Data Classification

All Northstar information is classified into one of four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted data includes customer payment card data and employee health records. Restricted data must always be encrypted, must never be stored on personal devices, and must never be sent from or to personal email accounts.

## 4. Payment Card Data

Staff must never write down, photograph, or store full customer card numbers. Card data may only be processed through approved POS terminals and payment systems.

## 5. Phishing

Employees must not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails. Suspicious emails must be reported using the "Report Phish" button in the email client or by forwarding them to security@northstar-retail.example.

## 6. Reporting Security Incidents

All security incidents, including lost or stolen devices, suspected malware, and possible data breaches, must be reported to Information Security within 1 hour of discovery. Incidents can be reported by email to security@northstar-retail.example or by phone on internal extension 4400.

## 7. Devices and Removable Media

Computers must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity, and employees must lock their screen manually whenever they leave their device unattended. USB storage devices are prohibited unless they are encrypted devices issued by IT. Only software from the Approved Software Catalogue may be installed; any other software requires a security review by Information Security.

## 8. Networks and Public Wi-Fi

When using public or home Wi-Fi, employees must connect through the NorthLink VPN before accessing any Northstar system. Confidential or Restricted data must not be viewed or worked on in public places such as cafés, airports, or trains.

## 9. Clean Desk

Documents containing Confidential or Restricted information must be locked away when not in use and must be shredded when no longer needed.

## 10. Security Awareness Training

All employees must complete security awareness training every year by 31 March. New employees must complete the training within 14 days of their start date.

## 11. Violations

Violations of these rules may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
