# Northstar Retail Systems — Customer Support Escalation Process

Document ID: NRS-CX-004
Owner: Customer Experience
Effective date: 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose

This process defines how customer complaints and issues are escalated so that every customer receives a timely and consistent response. It applies to all store associates, contact center agents, supervisors, and managers.

## 2. Support Tiers

Tier 1 — Frontline associates and contact center agents. Tier 1 staff should try to resolve the issue at first contact. Tier 1 staff may issue a goodwill credit of up to $50 without further approval.

Tier 2 — Shift Supervisors and Store Managers. An issue must be escalated to Tier 2 if the customer asks to speak to a manager, if the resolution involves more than $50, or if the issue has not been resolved within 24 hours. Store Managers may approve exceptions with a value of up to $200.

Tier 3 — Regional Customer Experience Manager. An issue must be escalated to Tier 3 if the exception value is above $200 and up to $1,000, if the customer makes a legal threat, if the media is involved, or if the case has already been escalated twice. Exceptions with a value above $1,000 require approval from the Director of Customer Experience.

## 3. Response Time Targets

Tier 2 must acknowledge an escalated case within 4 business hours. Tier 3 must acknowledge an escalated case within 1 business day. For every open escalated case, the customer must receive a status update at least every 2 business days until the case is resolved.

## 4. Escalation Steps

When escalating a customer issue, staff must follow these steps:

1. Acknowledge the customer's concern and apologize for the inconvenience.
2. Collect the order number, the receipt or proof of purchase, and the customer's contact details.
3. Log the case in CaseTrack within 30 minutes. Case numbers use the format CX-YYYY-NNNNN.
4. Assign the case to the correct tier based on the criteria in Section 2.
5. Give the customer the case number and the expected response time.
6. When the case is resolved, confirm the resolution with the customer and close the case. A customer satisfaction survey is sent automatically within 24 hours of closure.

## 5. Safety and Data Privacy Incidents

Issues involving customer safety or a possible data privacy breach bypass the normal tiers. Possible data privacy breaches, such as exposed customer card details or personal data, must be reported immediately to Information Security. Safety incidents must be reported immediately to the Health and Safety team.

## 6. Abusive Behaviour

If a customer becomes abusive, the associate may end the interaction after giving one clear warning. The associate must then inform their Shift Supervisor and record the incident in CaseTrack.
