# Northstar Retail Systems — Remote Work and Office Hours Policy

Document ID: NRS-HR-006
Owner: Human Resources
Effective date: 1 January 2026

## 1. Office Hours

The standard working week is 40 hours. The HQ office building is open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Friday. All HQ employees must be available during core hours, which are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, HQ employees may choose their start and finish times in agreement with their line manager.

Store-based employees work rostered shifts published by their Store Manager at least 2 weeks in advance.

## 2. Eligibility for Remote Work

Remote work is not available for store-floor roles or distribution centre roles. HQ employees become eligible for hybrid work after successfully completing their 3-month probation period.

## 3. Hybrid Work

Eligible HQ employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days, and all hybrid employees must work from the HQ office on those days.

## 4. Fully Remote Work

Fully remote work is granted only in exceptional circumstances. It requires approval from both the Department Head and HR, and each fully remote arrangement is reviewed every 6 months.

## 5. How to Request a Remote Work Arrangement

To request hybrid or fully remote work, the employee must submit the "Flexible Work Request" form in the PeopleHub portal. The line manager must respond to the request within 10 working days. Fully remote requests are then forwarded to the Department Head and HR for approval.

## 6. Equipment and Allowances

Hybrid and fully remote employees use their company-issued laptop for all work. Hybrid employees receive a one-time home office allowance of $300. The allowance must be claimed within 60 days of the remote work arrangement being approved, and original receipts must be submitted with the claim.

Employees approved to work remotely for 3 days per week, and fully remote employees, receive an internet stipend of $40 per month.

## 7. Security When Working Remotely

Remote employees must follow the Information Security Rules (NRS-SEC-005) at all times. In particular, employees must connect through the NorthLink VPN before accessing Northstar systems, and must not work on Confidential or Restricted data in public places.

## 8. Working From Abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 10 working days per calendar year. Working from abroad requires HR approval, and the request must be submitted at least 15 working days before travel.

## 9. Availability

During core hours, remote employees must be reachable on Northstar Chat and must respond to messages within 1 hour.

## 10. Overtime

Overtime for non-exempt employees must be approved in advance by the line manager.
