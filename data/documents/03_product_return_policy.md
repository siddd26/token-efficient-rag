# Northstar Retail Systems — Product Return Policy

Document ID: NRS-CX-003
Owner: Customer Experience
Effective date: 1 January 2026

## 1. Standard Returns

Most products can be returned within 30 days of purchase with a valid receipt. Returns with a receipt are refunded in full to the original payment method.

## 2. Electronics

Electronics, including laptops, tablets, phones, and headphones, have a return window of 14 days from the date of purchase. Electronics must be returned unopened, or with all original accessories and packaging. Opened electronics that are not defective are subject to a 15% restocking fee. No restocking fee is charged for defective electronics.

## 3. Returns Without a Receipt

Customers without a receipt may receive store credit for the lowest selling price of the item in the previous 60 days. A valid photo ID is required. Each customer is limited to 3 returns without a receipt in any 12-month period.

## 4. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned: clearance items marked "Final Sale", gift cards, personalized or engraved items, perishable food, and opened hygiene products such as cosmetics and earbuds.

## 5. Defective Products

Products that become defective through normal use can be returned within 1 year of purchase under the Northstar warranty. The customer may choose either a replacement or a refund.

## 6. Online Orders

Online orders can be returned in any store or by mail within 30 days of delivery. A free return label is provided when the item is defective or the wrong item was sent. For all other mail returns, a return shipping fee of $6.95 is deducted from the refund.

## 7. Refund Processing Times

Refunds to credit or debit cards are processed within 5 to 7 business days. Store credit is issued immediately.

## 8. Holiday Extended Returns

Purchases made between 1 November and 24 December can be returned until 15 January of the following year. The extended holiday window does not apply to electronics, which keep their 14-day window.

## 9. Northstar Rewards Gold Members

Customers with Gold tier membership in the Northstar Rewards program have a 45-day return window for most products. The 45-day window does not apply to electronics.

## 10. Exceptions

Store Managers may approve return exceptions (for example, a return outside the return window) with a value of up to $200. Exceptions above $200 must be escalated according to the Customer Support Escalation Process (NRS-CX-004).
