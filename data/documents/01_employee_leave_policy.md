# Northstar Retail Systems — Employee Leave Policy

Document ID: NRS-HR-001
Owner: Human Resources
Effective date: 1 January 2026

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains the types of leave available to employees of Northstar Retail Systems and how leave is requested, approved, and recorded. It applies to all permanent full-time and part-time employees at headquarters (HQ), distribution centres, and retail stores. Temporary and seasonal workers are covered by their individual employment contracts. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## 2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid annual leave for each completed month of service, which equals 21 days per leave year. Part-time employees accrue annual leave on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted hours.

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the new leave year; any carried-over days not used by that date are forfeited. Unused annual leave is paid out when employment ends.

## 3. Requesting Annual Leave

All annual leave must be requested through the PeopleHub portal. Requests for 3 or more consecutive working days must be submitted at least 10 working days before the first day of leave. Requests for 1 or 2 days must be submitted at least 2 working days in advance. The employee's line manager must approve or decline the request in PeopleHub within 3 working days of submission. Leave is not confirmed until it shows as "Approved" in PeopleHub.

## 4. Peak Season Blackout for Store Staff

Because of high customer demand, store-based employees may not take annual leave between 20 November and 2 January (the peak season blackout period). In exceptional circumstances, a store-based employee may take leave during the blackout period only with written approval from their Regional Manager. The blackout period does not apply to HQ employees.

## 5. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per leave year. Unused sick leave does not carry over. An employee who is unable to work because of illness must notify their line manager before 9:30 AM or before the start of their shift, whichever is earlier.

If a sickness absence lasts more than 3 consecutive working days, the employee must upload a medical certificate to PeopleHub within 2 working days of returning to work.

## 6. Parental Leave

Employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous service are eligible for paid parental leave. The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of leave at full pay. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of leave at full pay. Employees must notify HR of their intention to take parental leave at least 8 weeks before the expected start date.

## 7. Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member (spouse or partner, child, parent, or sibling) and up to 2 days of paid bereavement leave following the death of an extended family member (grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle, or in-law).

## 8. Unpaid Leave

Employees with at least 1 year of continuous service may request up to 30 days of unpaid leave per leave year. Unpaid leave requires approval from the HR Director.
